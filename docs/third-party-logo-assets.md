# Third-party technology logo assets

The compact technology badges use brand marks only to identify tools and platforms referenced in the portfolio. Their use does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

The following SVG files were adapted from the [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) project and are covered by its MIT license:

- `public/technology-logos/azure.svg`
- `public/technology-logos/oracle.svg`
- `public/technology-logos/powershell.svg`
- `public/technology-logos/visualbasic.svg`
- `public/technology-logos/windows.svg`

All product names, logos, and brands remain the property of their respective owners. Other compact brand marks are supplied by the project's existing `simple-icons` dependency or existing organization-logo assets.

The VWU education entry uses the current OeAD Vorstudienlehrgänge header mark from the official [Vienna programme website](https://vorstudienlehrgang.oead.at/en/vienna), retrieved on 2026-10-12 and displayed only for institutional identification:

- `public/organization-logos/vwu-oead.png`

The ÖSD certificate entry uses the organization image published in the structured metadata of the official [ÖSD website](https://osd.at/), retrieved on 2026-10-19 and displayed only for issuer identification:

- `public/organization-logos/osd.png`
